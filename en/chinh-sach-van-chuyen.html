Delivery times

  • Same city: 1 – 2 days.
  • Other provinces: 2 – 4 days via GHN, GHTK, Viettel Post.
  • Same-day express available within the city.

Shipping fees

  • Based on carrier rates, shown clearly in your quote.
  • Free shipping on orders from order amount.

Packaging

  • Multi-layer cartons with foam or honeycomb paper per item.
  • Fragile parts and small pieces are secured individually.

Dropship orders

  • Shipped straight to your customer with your label and card.
  • Tracking numbers are added to the shared order sheet as soon as parcels leave.
