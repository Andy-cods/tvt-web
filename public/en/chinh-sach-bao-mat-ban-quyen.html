Our privacy commitment

  • TVT never shares, sells or reprints customer designs for any third party.
  • Files are used only for the order you placed and only staff handling that order can access them.
  • After the order is complete you can ask us to delete your files.

Non-disclosure agreements

For business orders and unreleased products, TVT signs an NDA, yours or ours, before receiving files.

Intellectual property

  • You keep full rights to designs you provide.
  • For files TVT designs for you, usage rights pass to you once paid in full.
  • TVT may ask to photograph your product for our portfolio. You can say no.

What we will not print

  • Products infringing others’ copyright or trademarks without a licence.
  • Weapons, weapon parts and items banned under Vietnamese law.
  • Offensive or inciting content, or content targeting individuals or organisations.
