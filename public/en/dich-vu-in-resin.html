MSLA

High-detail resin printing

Resin printers use 8K–12K screens to cure liquid resin in layers just 0.03 mm thick. The result is crisp detail FDM cannot match, ideal for small parts that need finesse.

Get a quote Estimate price

Highlights

Why choose TVT for high-detail resin printing

Extreme detail

0.03 – 0.05 mm layers, layer lines practically invisible.

Several resins

Standard, tough ABS-like, and castable wax for jewellery.

Paint-ready

Smooth surface that needs only a light sand before painting.

Casting patterns

Castable resin burns out clean for jewellery workshops.

Specifications

Tolerance
