FAQ

Things you often ask.

Can’t find your question? Message us on Zalo and an engineer will reply during business hours.

Ordering

Can I print without a 3D file?

Yes. Send photos, a sketch or a description and our designers will model it and send a render for approval before printing. Design is quoted separately by complexity.

Which file formats do you accept?

STL, OBJ, 3MF or STEP. If a file has mesh errors or walls that are too thin, we fix small issues free and tell you before any larger changes.

Do you print a single piece?

Yes. There is no minimum order. Unit price drops as quantity grows.

How fast will I get a quote?

Within 2 business hours of receiving your file and a full description.

POD & dropship

How is POD different from bulk printing?

With POD you list products first and we print and ship only when an order comes in. No stock to buy, no inventory risk, and you can test many designs at once.

Will my customers know TVT made it?

No. The box, shipping label and card all carry your brand.

How do orders reach the studio?

Through a shared Google Sheet, or an API if you run your own website. We add tracking numbers to the same sheet.

Materials & quality

Can you print multiple colours?

Yes. Our multi-colour FDM printers handle up to 8 colours per print. For gradients or complex detail we recommend hand painting.

Are 3D printed parts durable?

It depends on the material. PETG and ASA work well outdoors, TPU is rubber-like, and carbon-fibre nylon handles load-bearing parts. Tell us the use and we will advise.

Are prints food safe?

We do not recommend 3D printed parts for direct food contact, because layer lines can trap bacteria.

Shipping & payment

How long until I receive my order?

Standard samples print in 24–72 hours, plus 1–4 days shipping. Same-day rush printing is available for a fee.

Do you issue VAT invoices?

Yes, on request. Please send invoicing details when you order.

How do I pay?

50% deposit on sample approval, balance on delivery. Single orders under 2 million ₫ can be paid cash on delivery.
