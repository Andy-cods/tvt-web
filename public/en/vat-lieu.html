Materials

Material shapes how it feels in your hand.

Each material has its own strengths in detail, strength and heat resistance. The table below helps you choose quickly.

MaterialDetailStrengthHeatBest for
PLA / PLA+FDM55°CDecor, models, gifts, indoor items
PETGFDM75°CBoxes, holders, everyday items, water resistant

Notes

More about each material.

PLA / PLA+

Easy to print, many colours, silk and matte variants. Not for direct sun or hot cars.

PETG

Tougher than PLA, resists water and mild chemicals. Better for everyday use.

ASA / ABS

ASA keeps its colour in the sun and handles heat well. The pick for outdoor parts.

TPU 95A

Rubber-like and unbreakable when bent. For parts that flex or absorb shock.

PETG-CF / Nylon-CF

Carbon-fibre filled for stiffness and a fine matte finish. Replaces light aluminium parts in many cases.

Standard 8K resin

The sharpest detail and smoothest surface. More brittle than FDM plastics, best for display.

ABS-like resin

Resin-level detail with more toughness. Good for parts that snap together.

Castable wax resin

Burns out with no ash, used for lost-wax casting in jewellery workshops.
